      SpaceFodder

      Lead a squad of armoured troopers against an alien swarm. Every recruit has a name. Not all of them come home.

      Left click to move · Right click to fire · Space to throw

      Field manual

      How to play

      Left click / hold
      Move the squad. Troopers follow the leader in a line.
      Right click / hold, or F
      Every trooper fires at the cursor.
      Space, E, middle click, or both buttons
      Leader throws a grenade or fires a rocket at the cursor.
      1 / 2, Q or Tab
      Pick grenades or rockets.
      Click names in the squad list, then X
      Split the marked troopers into a new team (up to three). No marks splits the team in half.
      C, or click a team name
      Command another team. Teams you are not commanding hold position and shoot back.
      J
      Join the team standing next to you.
      Click a vehicle
      The team walks over and climbs in if there are enough seats. R gets out.
      Esc or P
      Pause. M mutes sound.
      Ctrl + click
      Fires on one-button trackpads.
      Touch
      Tap to move. Fire and Bomb aim at the nearest threat.
      • Nests keep hatching aliens while you are near. Only explosives break them.
      • Explosions hurt everyone, your own troopers included. Throw long.
      • Troopers cannot shoot while wading. Acid pools burn and lava is a wall.
      • Fuel canisters explode when shot. Grenades flatten alien flora.
      • Colonists join the back of your line when a trooper reaches them. Walk them into the beacon.
      • Vehicles: the Crawler buggy (4 seats) runs aliens over, the Warden tank (3 seats) fires explosive shells, and the Skimmer (2 seats) hovers over water, acid and lava. Split the squad to fit.
      • Survivors are promoted after each win: tougher, faster, more accurate. The dead go on the wall.
